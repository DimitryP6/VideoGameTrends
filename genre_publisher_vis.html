Critic Score vs User Score Visualization for Two Genre Comparison

The above visualization is a mosaic plot between major video game companies and the amount of games they produced under that genre from the dataset. The genres are labeled by color in the legend, and the video game companies are labeled on the horizontal axis above the visualization.

The user can interact with the graph by hovering over a tile, which displays, aside from company and game genre, the percent of that game genre of the total games made for that company, and the top platform for that specific game company and the game genre.

Key Takeaways

  1. Nintendo, which as we saw from our previous plots has a large share in Japan, produces games primarily under the action, platform, and role-playing genres.
  2. Electronic arts, which as we saw from our previous plots has a large share in the Western market, produces game primarily under action, racing, shooter, and especially sports.
  3. By looking at the plot, we can tell that the most popular genres action, role-playing, shooter, and sports.
  4. Because some companies produce games under certain genres, that means that certain consoles come pre-packaged with games that fall under those genres more. In other words, the game a company develops depends on the console they want to market it to.
  5. Furthermore, because certain companies are in certain regions, that means that games come also pre-packaged with the companies that deploys them. In other words, video-game culture depends largely on the type of companies in that region. So, when creating a game, it is important to account for the region and the companies that exist there.

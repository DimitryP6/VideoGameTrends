Our Analysis

The above visualization is a mosaic plot between major video game companies and genres. Hovering over a mosaic states its percent share for the company of their genres, as well as the most used platform for that genre for the company that made the game.

From this visualization we can gather that certain companies prefer certain genres. For example, EA prefers sports games, while Nintendo prefers platform and role-playing games. This could mean that certain audiences where certain platforms have market share are exposed more to one type of game to another, which could impact video game culture in a region.

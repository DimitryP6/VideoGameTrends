Analyzing Trends in Sales and Their Tie To Video Game Genre

The above line chart visualization represents total sales in millions for a given year, where the lines are split by genre. In general, we can see that less popular genres experience less volatility, while genres in higher popularity experience more volatility. Furthermore, the graph altogether has a spike around the year 2007, after which it sinks.

The visualization has interactivity, where lines can be singled out by pressing on them. Furthermore, the user can brush and select certain years to consider, which shows a bar plot of genres and their total sales in millions over those selected years, which declusters the lines.

Key Takeaways

  1. From the line chart we can see that over the years, all genres have fallen in popularity while shooter, sports, and action games have risen on-net.
  2. The volatility for more popular genres can be explained by the fact that there is more competition amongst them, and so games that are popular one year for sports replace the popularity for games that may be in the action genre.
  3. However, low-popularity games have a low player-base, so their popularity is not affected much by competition.
  4. There is a sharp downfall in 2016, not because games have fallen, but probably because the dataset is outdated. More specifically, the dataset was scraped off of an older blog, and so users might have transferred to more modern gaming blog websites, such as Reddit for example.

While the plot is mainly static, the user can hover over a cell to see the specific sale count.

  1. We can tell that some consoles are geared more towards specific game genres, such as XBox for shooter and action, or even Playstation 3 console.
